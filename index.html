FAQ

Common questions

What exactly is AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation)?
+
AEO is to ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini what SEO is to Google. When someone asks an AI engine to recommend a product, service, or tool in your category, appearing in that recommendation is AEO. The rules are completely different from Google — AI engines favour authoritative, well-structured, comparison-rich content rather than keyword density and backlinks. PulseRank measures your AEO performance and tells you how to improve it.
How is this different from Otterly, Profound, or Peec?
+
All three are monitoring tools only. They show you where you're invisible and leave you to figure out why and what to do about it. PulseRank's Fix Engine generates specific, prioritised content recommendations tied to real citation gaps — giving you a clear action plan, not just a report of problems. We also include GA4 attribution (so you can prove ROI) and white-label reports for agencies, at a fraction of the cost of Profound.
Do I need to connect my website or install anything?
+
No installation required. You enter your domain and a list of queries relevant to your business, and PulseRank does the rest. GA4 connection is optional (for the attribution feature) and takes 2 minutes via standard OAuth. Nothing is installed on your site.
How does the Fix Engine actually work?
+
When PulseRank detects you're missing from a specific query, it analyses the pages currently being cited by AI engines for that query. It identifies patterns — page format, content type, FAQ structure, comparison format, schema markup — and generates a prioritised content brief for your site. Each fix includes the specific URL to update, the content to add or change, and an estimated citation improvement based on similar fixes we've tracked across our user base.
How often does PulseRank run queries against AI engines?
+
By default, your full query library runs once every 24 hours. Growth and Agency plans can trigger on-demand re-runs after making content changes, so you can verify fixes are being picked up. You get an email alert any time your AEO score changes by more than 5 points.
I run an SEO agency. How do client reports work?
+
Agency plan gives you white-label PDF reports with your agency's branding, logo, and domain. Each report contains the client's AEO score, engine breakdown, top competitor citations, and Fix Engine actions for the month. One-click generation. Most agencies send these as part of their monthly retainer reporting — it typically adds $200-500/month to what they can charge per client.
